Part looks

Every catalog part drawn from its shape by shapes.js, the way the planner draws it: library drawing, front view at 1.2 px/mm, and with &3d=1 the 3D model. The custom part maker's types come last (in 3D their printed geometry). Add ?ids=sbc7,ucgmax1 to show only those parts, ?ids=gen for the custom parts. Run a local server and open tools/looks.html.
