Notes

1 · Steps are the navigation, with words. The step bar moves into the 56 px top bar and each step says its state in text (“PoE missing”, “printer?”), not only as a colour. Today the states are colour plus a title tooltip.

2 · Export is secondary until the plan is ready. Today the brightest element on screen, from the first second and on an empty rack, is “Download build pack”. Here Export is an ordinary button; it turns primary only when Check has no problems.

3 · Library rows: one add target, honest chips. A 32 px “+” on the right (44 px on phones), height and watts as mono chips, a dashed chip for anything not sourced. Mount choice that changes power (PoE HAT) is a visible variant, not a hidden default.

4 · The rack shows usable space. Half-U slivers next to a ½U panel are hatched and labelled; open space says “1U open · room to grow”. Selection is a neutral 2 px outline, never the green.

5 · A status strip under the stage. The most important open problem, with its simplest fix as the button. It is the same item as in the build column, so fixing it in either place clears both.

6 · One “Next step” card on top of the build column. One sentence, one button. Today the column starts with a list of mixed problems, to-dos and info rows of equal weight.

7 · Problems: text first, button below. In a 360 px column, a button beside the text squeezes it to 7–8 lines. Icons separate kinds: filled ! = problem, amber ring = decision to make, dashed ≈ = estimate. Rack settings move into a section here; the centre column holds only the rack.

8 · Phone header: 60 px instead of 149 px. The current step and its state, a Steps sheet and Export. The six-segment bar shows progress without scrolling sideways.

9 · Phone actions at the thumb. A bottom bar with “Add part” and “Next: …”, 44 px high. The library is 1,400 px and readiness 2,500 px below the rack today.

10 · The library is a sheet over the rack. You see where the part lands while you add it; the confirmation appears in the sheet, next to your finger.

Colour roles. Green fills only actions (and the ✓ icon of a done step). Selection is neutral white/ink. Problems are red-orange, decisions amber, estimates dashed grey. The heat palette stays only in the Airflow view and heat chips.

Shapes. Buttons are 8 px rectangles (36 px desktop, 44 px phone). Pills are only for chips and segmented controls. Cards 12 px. So “Choose” (a button) no longer looks like “✓ chosen” (a status).

Type. Six sizes (12 / 13 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28), weights 400 / 600 / 700, body 14 px at 1.45. Geist Mono for every measurement: U, mm, W, °C.

Rhythm. A 4 px grid: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24. Card padding 16, gaps between groups 24, rows 8.
